# Brand guidelines (template)

Copy this file to `brand-guidelines.md` and edit it. The AI reads it before proposing ideas and writing
posts, so it is the place to say who you are and how you want to sound. Remove what does not apply.

## Who we are
One or two sentences: what the Page is about and who follows it.

## Audience
Who reads the posts, what they care about, what they already know.

## Voice and tone
- Friendly, clear, practical. Short sentences. No hype.
- Address the reader directly ("you").
- At most one emoji per post, and only if it adds meaning.

## What we post
- Tips: one useful, concrete idea the reader can use today.
- Myth vs fact: a common misunderstanding and the plain truth.
- Ideas to try: a small activity or step with a clear first action.

## Never
- Medical, legal or financial advice, cures, guarantees, or "100%" promises.
- Links in the post text (the link goes only in the first comment).
- Criticising other people, brands or Pages by name.
- Claims we cannot back up.

## Words we like / avoid
- Like: simple, small step, try.
- Avoid: miracle, secret, guaranteed.
